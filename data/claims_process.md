# Claims Handling Process

A home insurance policy can cover several main families of claims.

1. **Water damage** (leak, seepage, pipe burst, bathtub overflow, etc.).
2. **Fire / explosion**.
3. **Glass breakage** (windows, patio doors, verandas).
4. **Natural disasters** (floods, storms, earthquakes depending on local regulations and coverage).
5. **Theft, burglary, vandalism**.
6. **Third-party liability** (damage caused to a neighbor: a leak at home that affects the apartment below, a fire that spreads, etc.).
7. **Other property damage** (broken wall, partial collapse, falling object, damaged appliance, etc.).

For every claim, there are 3 main phases:

1.  **Declaration (policyholder side)** → What the policyholder must provide.

2.  **Analysis and investigation (insurer side)** → Verification, expert assessment, contact with the right parties.

3.  **Resolution (repair / claim closure).**

The processes for each claim family are listed below.

## 1. Water damage

-   **Expected from the policyholder:**
    -   Report the claim within 5 business days.
    -   Provide photos/videos of the damage, invoices if available.
    -   Fill in a **joint water damage report** with the neighbors/third parties concerned.

-   **Insurer process:**
    -   Verification of coverage.
    -   Contact the **partner plumber / service provider** to stop the leak.
    -   If the damage is significant → an **insurance expert** is appointed.
    -   Assessment of material and/or immaterial damage (loss of rent).

-   **Closure:**
    -   Either the insurer pays for the repairs directly,
    -   Or the policyholder is reimbursed on presentation of an invoice.

## 2. Fire / explosion

-   **Expected from the policyholder:**
    -   Call the fire brigade immediately.
    -   Report the claim within 5 days.
    -   File a police report if criminal activity is suspected.
    -   Provide an inventory of the destroyed items (photos, invoices).

-   **Insurer process:**
    -   Verification of the extent of coverage (fire, explosion, smoke).
    -   Appointment of a **fire expert**.
    -   Assessment of causes and damage (structure + furniture).
    -   Arrangement of **temporary accommodation** if the home is uninhabitable.

-   **Closure:**
    -   Compensation or repair through service providers.
    -   Assistance with restoring the home.

## 3. Glass breakage

-   **Expected from the policyholder:**
    -   Prompt declaration.
    -   Photos of the breakage + any invoice for a temporary replacement.

-   **Insurer process:**
    -   Verification of the "glass breakage" coverage.
    -   Direct referral to an **approved glazier**.

-   **Closure:**
    -   Quick repair (often without an expert assessment).
    -   Invoice paid directly by the insurer, or reimbursement.

## 4. Natural disasters (flood, storm, etc.)

-   **Expected from the policyholder:**
    -   Declaration within 10 days after publication of the natural disaster decree.
    -   Provide photos, invoices, inventory of the damaged items.

-   **Insurer process:**
    -   Verification of the ministerial decree (example: floods declared a "natural disaster").
    -   Dispatch of a **major-claims expert**.
    -   Overall estimate of repairs and losses.

-   **Closure:**
    -   Flat-rate or itemized compensation depending on the expert assessment.

## 5. Theft / burglary / vandalism

-   **Expected from the policyholder:**
    -   File a police report within 24 hours.
    -   Report the claim within 2 business days.
    -   Inventory of the stolen items (invoices, photos, warranties).

-   **Insurer process:**
    -   Verification of the "theft and vandalism" coverage.
    -   Request for the police report.
    -   If high-value items are involved → additional expert assessment.

-   **Closure:**
    -   Compensation based on replacement value or depreciated value.
    -   Help with securing the home (locksmith).

## 6. Third-party liability

-   **Expected from the policyholder:**
    -   Report the incident (e.g. a leak that damaged the neighbor's ceiling).
    -   Description of the circumstances + joint report if necessary.

-   **Insurer process:**
    -   Verification of liability and coverage.
    -   Contact with the victim's insurer.
    -   Settlement between insurers.

-   **Closure:**
    -   The victim is compensated by the insurer.
    -   Follow-up for any repairs.

## 7. Other property damage (broken wall, collapse, falling object…)

-   **Expected from the policyholder:**
    -   Declaration within 5 days.
    -   Photos / videos, purchase invoices, incident report if applicable.

-   **Insurer process:**
    -   Verification of the "building damage" or "accidental damage" coverage.
    -   If the damage is significant → an expert is appointed.

-   **Closure:**
    -   Compensation or direct handling.
