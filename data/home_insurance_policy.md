# Home Insurance Policy

**Policy number:** HAB-2025-123456  
**Policyholder:** John Smith – 12 Lilac Street, 75012 Paris

**Term:** 1 year, tacitly renewable

## 1. Included coverages

### Water Damage

-   Leaks, pipe bursts, seepage.
-   Overflow of household appliances (washing machine, dishwasher, etc.).
-   Damage caused to neighbors (third-party liability).

**Compensation cap:** €25,000  
**Deductible:** €150

### Fire / Explosion

-   Fire, explosion, implosion.
-   Accidental smoke.

**Compensation cap:** €100,000  
**Deductible:** €300

### Glass Breakage

-   Windows, glass doors, patio doors, verandas, fixed mirrors.

**Compensation cap:** €5,000  
**Deductible:** €50

### Theft / Vandalism

-   Theft with forced entry or assault.
-   Damage caused during an attempted theft.

**Compensation cap:** €20,000  
**Deductible:** €200

### Natural Disasters

-   Floods, storms, earthquakes (subject to a ministerial decree).

**Compensation cap:** €80,000  
**Statutory deductible:** €380

### Third-Party Liability

-   Property damage and bodily injury caused to third parties.

**Compensation cap:** €3,000,000  
**Deductible:** None

## 2. General Exclusions

-   Damage caused intentionally by the policyholder.
-   Normal wear and tear or lack of maintenance.
-   Items worth more than €5,000 without prior declaration.

## 3. Policyholder Obligations

-   Report any claim within the deadlines:
    -   5 business days (standard claim).
    -   2 business days (theft/vandalism).
    -   10 days after publication of a natural disaster decree.
-   Provide all necessary evidence (photos, invoices, police report where applicable).
-   Allow access to the expert appointed by the insurer.

## 4. Compensation Procedure

1.  Claim reported via the mobile app / online portal.
2.  Automatic verification of coverage.
3.  Expert assessment (remote or on site).
4.  Estimate of the amount of damage.
5.  Payment within 30 days of approval.
